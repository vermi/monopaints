About

Altanzul “Mono” Munkhjargal is a Mongolian-born, Chicago-based oil painter whose work explores memory, loss, and the relationship between human experience and the built environment. Raised in Ulaanbaatar’s Yarmag district in a family of artists, she learned to paint from her grandfather, whose home was later demolished amid the city’s rapid urban development, an experience that profoundly shaped her artistic vision.

Mono earned a Design Diploma (2010) and a Bachelor of Architecture (2018) from the Mongolian University of Science and Technology and is pursuing an MFA in Fine Art (Painting) at Academy of Art University, with anticipated graduation in Spring 2028. Her work has been featured in the Photo Message Photography Exhibition at iLoft Function House (2012), the Contemporary Art Exhibition at Bluemon Gallery (2013), and the Ulaanbaatar Street Art Festival (2013), all in Ulaanbaatar. In 2026, her painting Uncle’s Old House received the Best Landscape award at Academy of Art University’s Spring Show.

Drawing on her architectural training and personal experiences of urban transformation, her current thesis series, Faded Memory, depicts abandoned buildings slowly reclaimed by nature. Through expressive color, textured brushwork, and shifting natural light, she seeks to reveal the emotional histories embedded within deteriorating structures. As she writes in her thesis proposal, “These abandoned buildings are not empty shells.” For Mono, they are places where human memories endure, even as the physical world transforms around them.
